HOW EVPS SYSTEM WORKS

1

🚑 AMBULANCE

Emergency dispatch triggered with critical patient.

2

📍 GPS + ESP32

Transmitter captures real-time coordinates & speed.

3

📡 RF TRANSMIT

Priority request transmitted wirelessly to corridor.

Receiver acknowledges and locks green priority signal.

5

✓ AMBULANCE PASSES

Crossing event detected and priority safely cleared.

6

🟢 NORMAL CYCLE

Signal automatically restores to standard traffic timing.

HARDWARE PROTOTYPE ARCHITECTURE

Ambulance Unit (Transmitter)

  • Microcontroller: ESP32-WROOM-32 (240MHz Dual-Core)
  • GPS Module: u-blox NEO-6M (High refresh rate)
  • RF Transmitter: NRF24L01+ with PA/LNA (1.1 km range)
  • Operator Interface: Emergency button & TFT status

Traffic Junction Unit (Receiver)

  • Receiver Unit: ESP32 with NRF24L01 wireless node
  • Signal Switching: 3-Channel Solid-State / 5V Relay
  • Safety Failsafe: 45s Hardware Watchdog Timeout
  • Mesh Communication: Wi-Fi / MQTT Central Backhaul

Safety & Failsafe Assurance

  • Stale Request Rejection: Never locks green permanently
  • Failsafe Reversion: Automatically resets on signal drop
  • Sim vs Live Dual Mode: Physical bench testing ready
  • Audit Logging: Timestamped crossing records

 Hardware Diagnostic Reference & Field Checklist

          

            

              📍 GPS NEO-6M Checklist
              
• Baud: 9600 bps on GPIO 16 (RX) / GPIO 17 (TX).
• LED: Solid = Searching, Blinking = 3D Fix.
• Power: 3.3V / 5V DC filtered.

            

            

              📡 NRF24L01+ RF Transceiver
              
• CE: GPIO 22 | CSN: GPIO 21.
• Data Rate: RF24_250KBPS on Pipe 'EVPS1'.
• Capacitor: 10uF across VCC/GND recommended.

            

            

              🚦 3-Channel 5V Relay Wiring
              
• Relay 1 (Red): GPIO 12
• Relay 2 (Yellow): GPIO 13
• Relay 3 (Green): GPIO 14
• Active: HIGH / LOW Optocoupler Isolated.

            

            

              🛡️ Failsafe Watchdog Protocol
              
• Maximum Priority Hold: 45s.
• If ambulance halts or signal drops, normal traffic cycle auto-restores automatically.
